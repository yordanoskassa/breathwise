# Demo video — shot list (≤ 2:00)

Record the phone screen with QuickTime (iPhone → Mac by cable → *New Movie Recording* → pick the iPhone), and film the "phone over the sleeper" shot with a second camera. Only film people who have agreed to it; for a child, get a parent's OK.

| Time | Shot | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Dark room. Phone held over a sleeping person; the orb on screen breathes. | "Pneumonia kills more young children than any other infection. The first sign health workers check is fast breathing, counted by hand for a full minute. In trials, most counts on young infants were wrong." |
| 0:10–0:15 | Home screen: orb, "Measure breathing". | "Breathwise lets the camera do the counting." |
| 0:15–0:50 | **Hero shot:** measure screen. Heatmap tiles light up on the chest, brackets snap on, "Breathing found" is spoken, the counter ticks with each breath, the waveform scrolls. Cut to a finger tapping along ("Your taps: 12"). | "No touching, no wearable. It finds the breathing on its own, then counts for sixty seconds of clean signal." |
| 0:50–0:58 | Someone bumps the phone: the pill turns amber, "Movement: clock paused", then it recovers. | "If the child moves, the clock pauses instead of counting noise." |
| 0:58–1:15 | Result: the number counts up, then the verdict, the WHO cut-off gauge and the 60 s signal. Tick "chest pulls in", and it flips to **Seek care now**. | "It applies the WHO cut-off for the child's age and checks the danger signs." |
| 1:15–1:30 | How it works screen, then Settings, "No video ever leaves this phone". | "Everything runs on the phone. Each frame becomes 192 numbers and is thrown away. No video, no cloud." |
| 1:30–1:45 | Paywall: Family plan, Test Store purchase, then History trends and a shared PDF report. | "Counting is free forever. Families can upgrade for trends, doctor reports and night reminders, and every plan keeps it free for community health workers." |
| 1:45–1:55 | Switch to አማርኛ / Kiswahili; health-worker quick check with age chips. | "Five languages, built for the people who count breaths every day." |
| 1:55–2:00 | Orb + "Breathwise · open source". | "Breathwise." |

Tips: use a patterned blanket or top, light the room a little, and hold the phone about an arm's length away. Lock the phone against something (books or a tripod) for the cleanest lock-on.
